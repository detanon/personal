About the work

One throughline: make the space legible before it's built.

For two decades I've worked as an owner's representative and development strategist — feasibility, entitlement, consultant selection, budgeting — on portfolios worth billions. The plans and renders were never the product; they were how the thinking got made legible, so partners and approvers could decide with confidence. The same instinct shows up at every scale, from a 50-acre arena district down to a shelter I helped design and build by hand in the Arizona desert.
